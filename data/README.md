# Data room — SYNTHETIC

Every file here is generated by `src/generate_data.py` (seed 2026). Sparrow Pizza Co., Larkspur Restaurant Group, all vendors, franchisees, stores, employees and amounts are fictional.

**Conventions**
- NetSuite GL: debit/credit in the subsidiary's functional currency (SPZ_CA in CAD).
- HFM: periodic, USD, natural sign (revenue and expense positive; R1200 discounts negative).
- HFM loads are **YTD**, so anything posted after a month's extract appears in the following month.
- P&L translates at the monthly **average** rate (see Treasury rates file).
- Intercompany royalty: 5% of company restaurant sales, billed by SPZ_FR.

Scope: income statement only, July–September 2026, three entities (SPZ_US, SPZ_CA, SPZ_FR).
